Family income
10. Income of a family shall be taxed only by the territory in which the
member of that family who customarily receives that income in the first in-

Trust
11. Income of a trustee of any settlements or trusts, or estates or to an
executor of any estate of a deceased person, shall be taxed only by the terri-
tory in which the settlor or the person creating the trust is resident and to the
extent provided in the Fifth Schedule to this Act.
