Nigeria Tax Act, 2025
A  540
 2025  No. 7
FOURTH SCHEDULE
 Section 58
INDIVIDUALS' INCOME TAX RATES
After the relief allowance and exemptions had been granted in accordance with sub-
section of (1) of section 30 of this Act, the taxable income ascertained shall be taxed at the
following rates –
(a) First N800,000 at 0% ;
(b) Next N2,200,000 at 15% ;
(c) Next N9,000,000 at 18% ;
(d) Next N13,000,000 at 21% ;
(e) Next N25,000,000 at 23% ; and
(f) Above N50,000,000 at 25%.
