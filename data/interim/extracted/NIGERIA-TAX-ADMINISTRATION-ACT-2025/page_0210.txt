Nigeria Tax Act, 2025
A  594
 2025  No. 7
FOURTEENTH SCHEDULE
Section 59(6)
DEFENCE AND SECURITY INFRASTRUCTURE FUND
 Establishment of Fund
1.—(1) There is established a Fund known as the Defence and
Security Infrastructure Fund (the Fund) for the implementation of a national
security scheme and the development of defence industry and infrastructure
in Nigeria.
(2) There shall be paid and credited to the Fund established under this
schedule, 10% of the development levy imposed in section 160 of this Act.
(3) The Fund created under this schedule shall be used exclusively for
operational purposes to enhance security in Nigeria.
 Management of Fund
2.—(1) The Fund is to be managed jointly by the following security
agencies and bodies:
(a) Nigerian Army, Nigerian Navy and Nigerian Airforce, established
under the Armed Forces Act, Cap. A20, Laws of the Federation of
Nigeria, 2004 ;
(b) Nigerian Police Force established by Police Act, Cap. P19, Laws of
the Federation of Nigeria 2004 ;
(c) Department of State Security (DSS) established under the National
Security Agencies Act, Cap. N74, Laws of the Federation of Nigeria,
2004 ;
(d) Nigerian Security and Civil Defence Corps (NSCDC) established
under the Nigerian Security and Civil Defence Commission Act, No. 6,
2007 ;
(e) Nigerian Forest Security Service (NFSS) established under the
Nigerian Forest Security Service Act 2025 ;
(f) Defence Intelligence Agency (DIA) established under Decree 19 of
1986 ; and
(g) Office of the National Security Adviser established under the
National Security Agencies Act of 1986.
(2) The monies due to each agency as prescribed under paragraph 3 of
this Schedule shall be paid directly to the agency's account with the Central
Bank of Nigeria.
(3) Monies from the Fund shall be used by the agencies to fulfil the
performance of functions and obligations as provided under the establishment
laws of the agencies, including obligations on maintenance, reporting and
accountability of funding.
