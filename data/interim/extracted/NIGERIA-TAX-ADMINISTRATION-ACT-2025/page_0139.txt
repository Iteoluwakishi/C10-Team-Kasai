             Transitional rules for capital allowances
23.—(1) Where a capital allowance has been granted in respect of an
asset before the commencement of this Act, the remaining basis periods in
which allowance may be made under this Part, shall be the number of years of
assessment for which allowance is to be made under this Part less the number
of years of assessment for which allowance has previously been made.
(2)  Where capital allowances were granted, in respect of an asset before
the commencement of this Act, for years of assessment equal to or greater
than the number of years allowable under this Act, a single allowance shall be
granted for the residue upon the commencement of this Act provided that
there shall be recorded in the capital allowance computation schedule for sta-
tistical purposes, 1% of the qualifying capital expenditure until the asset is
disposed which being a notional amount shall not increase or reduce the amount
of capital allowance claimable under this Act.
