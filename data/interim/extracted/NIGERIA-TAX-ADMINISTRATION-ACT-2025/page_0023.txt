18.—(1) Where a non-resident person carries on the business of transport
by sea or air, and any ship or aircraft owned, leased or chartered by it calls at
any port in Nigeria, the non-resident person is chargeable to tax on the profits
arising from the carriage of passengers, mails, livestock or goods shipped from,
or loaded into an aircraft or ship, in Nigeria.
(2) The provisions of subsection (1) of this section shall not apply to
passengers, mails, livestock or goods which are brought into Nigeria solely for
trans-shipment or for transfer from one aircraft to another or between an
aircraft and a ship.
